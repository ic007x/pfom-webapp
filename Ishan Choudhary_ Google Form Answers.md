## Q2) What Additional Features Did You Identify or Implement?

### 1. Major Additions

**Live Mill Capacity & Ready Stock Ticker:** The header shows up-to-date mill production numbers (420 MT) together with available stock levels (180 MT) to ensure continuous bulk supply availability.

**Dynamic Bulk Price & Freight Estimator:** Users can adjust a quantity slider between 5 MT and 200+ MT whileselecting delivery zones to obtain immediate volume discount information and freight cost estimates.

**Zero-Middleman Margin Visualizer:** The tool presents two separate columns which demonstrate how broker operations increase supply chain costs by 13% to 18% through their commission fees and how mills sell their products directly at their production facilities.

**Certified Lab Sample Kit Request Workflow:** Industrial B2B buyers can use this digital system to request physical samples and sealed NABL Certificate of Analysis (CoA) reports before they finalize their substantial contract agreements.

**Session-Clearing AI Sales Assistant:** The technical assistant contains industrial oil specifications which include FFA levels and pungency and sortex purity and it deletes all chat messages when a user session ends for security purposes.

### 2. Nuances Additions

**Bilingual English/Hindi Vernacular Support:** The top navigation bar features an instant language switcher whichserves traditional mandi traders and regional procurement teams by displaying their preferred language.

**Direct WhatsApp B2B Handoff Integration:** The web portal connects users to the sales head through a single tap which enables them to send messages for traditional commodity negotiations that focus on building relationships.

**"Mill-Gate Freshness" Scarcity Indicators:** Micro-copy messages inform customers about current harvesting periods and limited production runs which create a sense of urgency that leads buyers to request quotes quickly.

**Tactile 2D Neo-Brutalist Visual Hierarchy:** The design uses three colors which include a `#fafafa` canvas and `#ffea01`highlights and `#000000` structure to show flat geometric typography and an elevated 3D/shadow-lifted quotation button that serves as a conversion focus point.
